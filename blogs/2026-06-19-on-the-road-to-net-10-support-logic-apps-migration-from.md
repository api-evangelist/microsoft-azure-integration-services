Covers migrating Azure Logic Apps Standard from the in-process (in-proc) hosting model to the out-of-process (out-of-proc) hosting model as part of preparing for .NET 10 support. The change modernizes the Logic Apps runtime hosting architecture to align with current .NET platform requirements.
